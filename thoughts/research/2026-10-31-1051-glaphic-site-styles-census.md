## Topic 0 — the styling contract the two repos actually run

The library exposes styling through four channels (glaphicui.md + the component sources):

1. **Tier-2 knobs** — custom properties on `:host` with design defaults, "names state WHAT THEY CHANGE" (glaphicui.md). Sites consume them as ordinary token rules.
2. **Parts** — `part="role"` names, "the styling API: consumers style through ::part" (lit.md). The marquee's own sheet **documents** the pattern: "A page styles/breaks them responsively through the parts (::part(rail-top) / ::part(rail-bottom)) instead of overlaying twin bars outside the band" (glaphic-marquee.ts:128-131).
3. **State attributes** — e.g. `glaphic-pool-menu[open]`, `glaphic-wipe[writing]` (wipe.ts:232) — the interop channel; pages read them, they are not style hooks.
4. **Light-DOM adoption for optical components** — `GlaphicLightElement` renders into the document and adopts a tag-scoped sheet (`glaphic-card .plate` style) exactly once (base/light-dom.ts:1-40). The site's outer rules therefore legitimately cascade against the adopted sheet by specificity — Projects.tsx:203's comment records this as "over the component's adopted sheet".

Token pinning runs one way: the site re-binds the **mono** Tier-1 channel to its real faces (`--glaphic-font-family-mono: "IBM Plex Mono"…`, index.css:1). The **display/sans** channels are UNPINNED (tokens.css:108-110) — harmless today because the library's defaults ("Space Grotesk"/"Inter") are the site's own faces, and only glaphic-collapse, ink-stroke, accordion-item, cast consume them (repo grep).

## Topic 3 — what stays with the page, and why

- **Host-layout**: margin/position/z/height/size-seats place EACH instance in the page's composition; the library owns the component's inside, the page owns where it sits. (The marquee hosts ×4, plates ×3, tape margins, chip alignment, legal tape's display.)
- **Page-flag choreography**: `.ab-on` / `.lt-in` / `.ln-in` / `.ptd-enter-on` gate every entrance (marquee drops, chip pops, bloom reveal, tape-in). The gate is page state; the reduce paths necessarily ride the page's own rules too.
- **Blend-boundary placement**: `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on the marquee hosts is the page's decision about WHICH boundary holds the blend — the library's own blend chapter assigns this to the author.
- **Author nodes**: slotted anchors/pool-menu links, the slide's img, the chip icon spans, `.ln-bloom-copy` — lit.md's law; the site styling its own children is not a component style.
- **The site's layer/interaction contract**: the wipe's fixed z seat, the band's pointer-events re-arming.
- **Outer-tree beaten re-states**: the slide-image fill and the pool-menu link colors exist only because Tailwind preflight plays in the OUTER tree where the library's `::slotted` cannot win — no library change can fix these; the page must carry them.
